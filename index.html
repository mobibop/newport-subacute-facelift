About us

Caring for your loved ones like family

Newport Sub-acute Healthcare Center is a long-term care provider in Costa Mesa. Our mission is to care for your loved ones as if they were our own, through patient-centered, comprehensive care. Every staff member is carefully selected and continuously trained, and we're always open for assessments and consultations to design a care plan around your family's needs.
